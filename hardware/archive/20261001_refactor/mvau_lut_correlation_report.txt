=======================================================================
4) Testing the user-proposed formula:
   mvu_lut = c0 + c3*MH + c1*P*(A + mult_luts + addertree_luts + acc_luts)
   (M=1, mult_luts=0 since all rows are force_dsp)
=======================================================================
current formula as-is (c0=300, c1=1.1, UNFIT, force_dsp)
    R^2 = -0.4040   RMSE=13012   (worse than predicting the mean)

c0 + c1*X_combined  (X = P*(A + addertree_luts + acc_luts), no MH)
    R^2 = 0.0001   RMSE=10981   <- essentially zero signal

c0 + c3*MH + c1*X_combined   (proposed formula, OLS-fit)
    R^2 = 0.3955   RMSE=8538
    c3*MH          = 669.8
    c1*X_combined  = 2.88

c0 + c3*MH + c1*(P*A) + c2*(P*(addertree_luts+acc_luts))  [separate slopes]
    R^2 = 0.6759   RMSE=6252
    c3*MH                    = 312.6
    c1*(P*A)                 = 181.4
    c2*(P*(addertree+acc))   = -3.30   <- NEGATIVE, wrong sign physically

c0 + c1*(P*A) + c2*(P*(addertree_luts+acc_luts))   [no MH]
    R^2 = 0.6144   RMSE=6819

c0 + c3*MH + c1*(P*A)   [drop addertree/acc structural terms entirely]
    R^2 = 0.6692   RMSE=6316

CONCLUSION: the proposed formula, evaluated with a single shared
coefficient across A/addertree_luts/acc_luts, underperforms badly
(R^2=0.40 with MH, ~0 without) because addertree_luts/acc_luts scale with
SIMD, and SIMD is NEGATIVELY correlated with real_LUT in this dataset
(SIMD alone: -0.41). Sharing one coefficient across a positively-correlated
term (A) and negatively-correlated terms (addertree/acc) cancels most of
the signal. Even allowing the structural term its own free coefficient,
the fit assigns it a NEGATIVE weight (-3.3) -- physically backwards -- and
only nudges R^2 from 0.669 to 0.676, not a meaningful improvement.
BEST SIMPLE, PHYSICALLY-SANE MODEL SO FAR: real_LUT ~ MH + PE*act_bits
(R^2=0.669).

=======================================================================
5) Testing: mvu_lut = c0 + c1*M*P*(mult_luts + c2*addertree_luts + c3*acc_luts)
   (M=1, mult_luts=0 since force_dsp everywhere -- c1 not separately
   identifiable from c2/c3 in a linear fit, equivalent to 2 free combined
   coefficients on P*addertree_luts and P*acc_luts)
=======================================================================
c0 + c1*P*(mult_luts + c2*addertree_luts + c3*acc_luts)
    R^2 = 0.5455   RMSE=7403.4
    c0 (intercept)              2287.96
    coef on P*addertree_luts     -6.02   (wrong-signed)
    coef on P*acc_luts           90.90

PE*act_bits + MH   (comparison baseline)
    R^2 = 0.6692   RMSE=6315.8
    intercept              -3096.91
    coef on PE*act_bits       176.45
    coef on MH                337.10

P*addertree_luts + P*acc_luts + PE*act_bits + MH  (all 4 terms combined)
    R^2 = 0.7011   RMSE=6003.6
    intercept           -806.30
    P*addertree_luts      -2.53   (flips negative once PE*act_bits/MH present)
    P*acc_luts           -95.71   (also flips negative)
    PE*act_bits          374.23
    MH                   352.21

CONCLUSION: PE*act_bits + MH (R^2=0.669) remains the best simple,
physically-coherent model. addertree_luts/acc_luts terms consistently
flip sign or add only marginal R^2 once PE*act_bits/MH are present --
not solid independent evidence of a real additive structural effect at
this sample size (n=89).

=======================================================================
6) Testing: ADD PE*act_bits + MH to the EXISTING formula (not swap)
   i.e. mvu_lut = c0 + c1*P*(mult_luts+addertree_luts+acc_luts)
                     + c4*(PE*act_bits) + c5*MH
=======================================================================
current formula alone: c0 + c1*P*(mult+addertree+acc)
    R^2=0.0090   (basically useless on its own once properly fit)

current formula + PE*act_bits (added)
    R^2=0.6144

current formula + MH (added)
    R^2=0.3902

current formula + PE*act_bits + MH (both added)
    R^2=0.6759   coefs: intercept=-1141.6, structural=-3.30, PE*act_bits=181.4, MH=312.6

PE*act_bits + MH alone (structural term dropped entirely)
    R^2=0.6692

CONCLUSION: adding both terms to the existing formula only nudges R^2 from
0.6692 to 0.6759 (+0.0067) -- negligible. The structural term's own fitted
coefficient is NEGATIVE (-3.30), i.e. regression wants to SUBTRACT it, not
add it, once PE*act_bits/MH are present. The addertree_luts/acc_luts term
isn't earning its keep -- dropping it in favor of plain PE*act_bits + MH is
simpler and essentially as accurate.
